## 总原则

- **时代基底全大陆统一**：类 19 世纪工业革命——煤气灯、马车、砖石结构、蒸汽机械全球通用；服装廓形统一为维多利亚时代基底（礼帽、束腰、三件套、长裙），各国只替换地域元素。
- **殖民地双层规则**：拜朗/帕斯/高地等被殖民或瓜分地区，占领者风格与本地风格分层并列（prompt 用 `beside / next to`）。
- **串味即失败**：鲁恩出棕榈、弗萨克出陶瓦、费内波特出洋葱顶都是串味，重跑。

## 鲁恩王国（≈维多利亚英国）

- **建筑注入块**：`Victorian British: red brick terraces, cream Portland stone civic facades, slate gable roofs with chimney stacks, bay windows, black cast-iron railings, dark green or black doors`
- **服设矩阵**：
  - 男：礼服 `white tie tailcoat, top hat, white gloves`；日常 `three-piece suit with pocket watch chain, bowler hat`；风衣 `dark trench coat, half top hat`；工作 `flat cap, suspenders, rolled-up sleeves`
  - 女：礼服 `evening gown with long gloves, tiara`；日常 `bustle day dress, feathered hat, lace parasol`；工作 `maid uniform with white apron, or factory dress with shawl`
- **植被资产清单**：`English oak`、`London plane tree`、`trimmed boxwood hedge`、`rose bed`、`hydrangea in terracotta pot`
- **美食清单**：`fish and chips`、`roast beef with yorkshire pudding`、`meat pie`、`scone with jam`、`cup of black tea`、`mug of ale`
- **地标示例**：哥特复兴大教堂（黑夜女神，`Gothic Revival cathedral, twin spires, rose window`）、蒸汽火车总站（`Victorian train station, brick clock tower entrance with glass-and-iron arched train shed`）、皇宫（`grand classical stone palace, columned portico`）、首相府（`modest terraced government townhouse, black door with brass number`）、证券交易所（`stock exchange with Corinthian columned portico`）
- **色彩板**：红砖 / 奶白石材 / 深绿门窗 / 灰板岩
